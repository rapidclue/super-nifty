Practical Rules

DeltaDIRECTION

ATM ≈ 0.50. Directional: ITM delta >0.70. Short strangles: delta 0.15–0.20/side. Crash protection: puts delta ≥0.40.

GammaEXPIRY RISK

ATM near expiry = extreme gamma. 50-pt move shifts delta 0.3→0.7 in minutes. Never sell naked ATM on expiry AM.

Theta CurveWEEKLY

Thu: 10–15% · Fri: 15–20% · Mon: 25–35% (exit longs EOD!) · Tue: 40–60% final hours. ATM Theta=-6 → ₹390/day/lot.

VegaVOLATILITY

Long = benefits from ↑IV. Short = benefits from ↓IV. VIX falling + long options = theta AND vega work against you.
