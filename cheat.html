Options Data

OI, PCR & IV Landscape

  • Previous day OI build-up at key strikes (max pain zone)
  • PCR (Put-Call Ratio) — <0.7 bearish, 0.7–1.0 neutral, >1.0 bullish
  • IVP (IV Percentile) — >60 = high premium, favor selling; <30 = cheap, favor buying
  • ATM Straddle price → expected move = straddle × 0.6
  • GEX (Gamma Exposure) — positive = mean-reverting, negative = trending
  • FII/DII previous day net — FII selling + DII buying = choppy; aligned = trending

Day-Type Classification

Never commit to a day-type before 09:45 IST. The first 30 minutes establish the opening range and initial context. Reclassify at 11:00 and 13:00.

Rule: Wait until 09:45 IST. The opening 15–30 min candle defines the opening range. Day-type hypothesis forms only after price interacts with VWAP + Opening Range boundaries.
Trending Day

Directional Momentum

Detection Signals

Gap >0.5% that doesn't fill in 30 min · Price stays one side of VWAP all session · Negative GEX · IVP <40 · PCR extreme (<0.65 or >1.3) · FII + DII aligned · Opening range break with volume surge >1.5× average

Playbook

Buy ATM/slight OTM options in direction · Trail SL using 5-min swing lows/highs · Don't counter-trade · Add on pullbacks to VWAP · Hold winners till 14:30+

Range-Bound Day

Rotational / Balanced

Detection Signals

Small gap (<0.3%) that fills quickly · Price oscillates around VWAP · Positive GEX · Narrow CPR · IVP 40–60 · PCR near 0.85–1.05 · No sector leadership — breadth flat

Playbook

Sell OTM options (iron condors / strangles) · Fade extremes near PDH/PDL · Book profits quickly — 50% of premium target · Avoid breakout chasing · Cut if range breaks with volume

Mean-Reversion Day

Gap-Fill / Snap-Back

Detection Signals

Gap >0.5% that begins filling within first 15 min · Price crosses VWAP within 30 min · Positive GEX · IVP >50 (overpriced) · Opening range break fails · FII/DII divergent flows

Playbook

Fade the gap — trade toward gap-fill level (PDC) · Sell premium on gap side · Tight SL beyond gap extreme · Target is VWAP then PDC · Exit if gap-fill fails at 50% retrace

Risk Management Rules

These are non-negotiable. Break a risk rule = shut down for the day.

Hard Limits

Non-Negotiable Rules

Per-Trade Max Loss≤ 1% of Capital

No single trade risks more than 1% of total trading capital. Calculate before entry: SL pts × 65 × lots ≤ 1% capital

Daily Max Loss≤ 2% of Capital

Hit 2% daily drawdown → stop trading. No exceptions. No revenge trades. Close all positions.

Weekly Max Loss≤ 5% of Capital

3 losing days in a row OR 5% weekly drawdown → pause for the week. Review all trades.

Max Concurrent Positions≤ 3

No more than 3 open positions at once. Each must have independent thesis. No doubling down on losers.

Max Lots (Per Trade)SIZE LIMIT

Scale based on confidence: A+ setup = full size, B setup = 60%, C setup = 30%. Never exceed max lots from sizing formula.

Position Sizing

Sizing Formula & Adjustments

Base FormulaFORMULA

Lots = (Capital × 1%) ÷ (SL in points × 65)

Example: ₹10L capital, 30pt SL → Lots = (10,000) ÷ (30 × 65) = 5.1 → 5 lots

AdjustmentsMODIFIERS

Event day: × 0.5 · Expiry day: × 0.5 · Losing streak (3+): × 0.5 · A+ confluence (≥5/6): × 1.0 · B setup (3–4/6): × 0.6 · After daily target hit: × 0.3 (house money only)

Scaling InOPTIONAL

Enter 60% at initial signal. Add 40% on confirmation (e.g., VWAP hold after breakout). Never average losers. Only add to winners with confirming price action.

Exit Rules

Exits matter more than entries. Pre-define every exit before you enter.

Stop-Loss Rules

When to Cut

Initial SLMANDATORY

Set at entry. Based on structure (below swing low / above swing high on 5-min). Never a round number — always give 2–3pts buffer. SL order placed immediately.

Time-Based SL15 MIN RULE

If trade hasn't moved in your favor within 15 minutes, exit at market or tighten SL to breakeven. Dead trades = opportunity cost.

Day-Type InvalidationEXIT ALL

If your day-type classification flips (trending → range), exit all positions based on old thesis. Reassess from scratch.

VWAP InvalidationCUT

Long trade: if price closes below VWAP on 5-min candle, exit. Short trade: close above VWAP = exit. This overrides structure-based SL.

Profit-Taking Rules

When to Book

Partial Exit: 50% at 1:1 R:RBOOK

Book half at 1:1 risk-reward. Move SL to breakeven on remainder. Locks in guaranteed profit.

Trail: Swing-BasedTRAIL

Remaining 50%: trail SL below each new 5-min swing low (longs) or above swing high (shorts). Let trends run.

Full Exit: R:R or LevelTARGET

Full exit at 2:1 R:R OR at key level (PDH, PDL, OI wall, round number). Whichever comes first.

Time Exit: 14:45 ISTMANDATORY

No overnight holding for intraday options. Everything flat by 15:15 latest. Start winding down at 14:45.

Common Mistakes & Rules

A catalog of recurring mistakes and the hard rules that prevent them. Review weekly.

Emotional Mistakes

Psychology Traps

Revenge TradingKILLER

Taking an impulsive trade after a loss to "win it back." Rule: After any loss, wait 15 minutes. If daily loss >1.5%, stop completely.

FOMO EntryKILLER

Entering because "it's running away." Rule: If you missed the entry, wait for a pullback. No chasing. There's always another trade.

Moving Stop-LossKILLER

Widening SL to avoid being stopped out. Rule: SL is sacred. If it hits, accept it. Moving SL = adding to losers with extra steps.

OvertradingWARNING

Taking too many trades to feel productive. Rule: Max 5 trades per day. Quality over quantity. Flat is a position.

Technical Mistakes

Execution Errors

Wrong Strike / ExpiryCHECK

Double-check strike price AND expiry before clicking buy. On Dhan: verify Tuesday expiry. Fat finger = instant loss.

Ignoring SpreadCHECK

Wide bid-ask spread in OTM options eats into edge. Rule: Don't trade options with spread >5% of premium.

No SL PlacedKILLER

"I'll watch it" is not a risk management strategy. Rule: SL order must be placed within 30 seconds of entry. No exceptions.

Trading in Low LiquidityCHECK

Deep OTM options on non-expiry days have poor liquidity. Rule: Only trade strikes within ±5 of ATM unless on expiry day.

The #1 Rule: Capital preservation beats profit-seeking. Your job is to survive long enough for your edge to compound. One blown account erases months of gains. When in doubt, sit out.
